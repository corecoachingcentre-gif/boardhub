Board Hub is an educational support platform designed for Class 9 to 12 students of 
CBSE and MP Board. We provide organized study material, topic-wise notes, past year 
papers, practice sets, online classes, and revision guidance to help students score
better in school and board examinations.

We focus on:
✔ Concept clarity with simple explanations  
✔ Exam-oriented preparation strategies  
✔ Accessible study notes and PYQs  
✔ Guidance for self-study and revision  
✔ Subject support from Class 9–12 (Science, Maths, English & more)

Our goal is to empower every student with confidence and quality learning resources.
